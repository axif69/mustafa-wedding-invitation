An interactive, 3D animated digital wedding invitation card crafted for **Mustafa & Zainab** (19th January 2027 at Hussaini Masjid Sahen, Bhopal).

## ✨ Features

- **3D Royal Palace Door & Knocker Interaction:**
  - Double doors with traditional arabesque filigree and brass knocker.
  - Realistic multi-tone acoustic knocking sound powered by the **Web Audio API** (no external audio downloads needed!).
  - Haptic mobile feedback on knock.
  - Doors swing open in full 3D perspective to reveal a golden sanctum glow.
- **Ambient Canvas Particles:**
  - Soft pink and crimson rose petals gently falling alongside shimmering golden stardust.
- **Background Wedding Instrumental Music:**
  - Royal harmonium/tanpura & shehnai melody synthesized via Web Audio with a one-click mute/unmute floating controller.
- **Live Countdown Timer:**
  - Real-time countdown to Tuesday, 19th January 2027 (8:00 PM IST).
- **Personalized Guest Links (`?to=Guest+Name`):**
  - Generates custom URLs for each family or guest (e.g. `?to=Uncle+Rashid+and+Family`).
  - Interactive "Create Personalized Link" tool with direct WhatsApp sharing built-in.
- **One-Tap Guest Convenience Hub:**
  - **Add to Google Calendar / Apple Calendar** with pre-filled venue and time details.
  - **Google Maps Navigation** directly to Hussaini Masjid Sahen, Bhopal.
  - **One-Tap WhatsApp RSVP & Direct Call** to the hosts.
- **Dual-View Presentation:**
  - High-Fidelity Interactive Calligraphy Card View.
  - High-Resolution Original Printed Cards Gallery (with date retouched to 2027) with 1-click download.
